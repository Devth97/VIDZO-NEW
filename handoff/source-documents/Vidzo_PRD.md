# Reference document — not agent instructions

VIDZO  /  PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Product Requirements Document

01  Product mandate

Vidzo.tech | B2B reference-to-original advertising studio. Convert an authorized reference into a distinct commercial ad using customer products, logos and original characters. No low-quality video tier. Influencer tools deferred. Version 0.1; 25 November 2026.

02  Market and differentiation

Agencies, D2C brands and dropshippers are initial buyers; jewelry, apparel, electronics and cosmetics are example verticals, not separate products. Creatify documents variable charges for Ad Clone; Higgsfield acknowledges stuck jobs and failed generation troubleshooting. Compete on predictable quote, visible approvals, product fidelity and targeted revisions, not merely access to models. Arcads is a UGC benchmark; its authenticated workflow remains untested. [1–2]

03  P1 functional requirements

P01 Account and project: email sign-in; private workspace; resume a draft. P02 Upload: MP4/MOV, 100 MB, 5–30 seconds reference; validate rights, type and duration. P03 Analysis: summary, hook, shot times, camera, character roles, product interactions, CTA and confidence; user can correct. P04 Characters: zero for no people; three alternatives for a single role; two per role for multi-character references (maximum two roles at launch). Each sheet includes front, profile, three-quarter and full-body views; select, redraw one candidate at quoted cost, save approved identity. Never derive a recognizable replacement likeness without permission. P05 Brand kit: product photos, logo, color, optional font and claims; one primary product; manual entry for website or offer. P06 Creative brief: objective, audience, language, tone, CTA and differences from reference. P07 Storyboard: up to four scenes, 10–15 seconds output; review every frame, edit prompt, lock scene and request individual regeneration. P08 Generate: professional/premium quote, confirm credits, progress, clip previews, retry one scene. P09 Export: vertical 1080×1920 MP4 delivery, disclose native resolution and any upscale; exact logos added as overlays. P10 Wallet: prepaid credits, purchase history, reservation/refund visibility. P11 Admin: job diagnostics, spending limits, manual review and support.

04  Acceptance and quality gates

P02 rejects unsupported or corrupt media without debit. P03 timestamps are within source duration; uncertain role count editable. P04 selected character version is persistent and all other choices remain available. P05 original asset files are immutable. P07 changing a scene invalidates only its dependent clip and export. P08 duplicate clicks never create duplicate charges; technical failures release unsettled credits; QA rejects obvious product distortion before delivery. P09 export has no reference audio/marks, readable CTA, correct aspect and no missing frames. P10 reconciliation matches payment and wallet events. Human review of likeness, product and claims required for beta. No promise of virality or ROAS.

05  Commercial rules

Pilot price hypothesis Rs 1,499 for 10–15 seconds; quote premium/complex scenes separately. Base credit value Rs 10; test 100 credits at Rs 999 and 250 at Rs 2,499 after measuring cost. Paid redraws show cost before confirmation. Provider failure is not a paid creative redraw. Do not promise unlimited retries or perfect fidelity. Free analysis limited to one per verified account/day; no free full video. Purchased balances remain delivery liabilities. Terms disclose retention, refund policy and commercial rights.

06  Success metrics and release plan

Targets, not forecasts: three paid pilots before major SaaS spend; 10 paid buyers by day 30–45; 30% repeat purchase within 30 days of delivery; >=85% final acceptance, >=60% contribution margin after variable costs and payment fees. Track upload→analysis→storyboard→quote→payment→export, first-pass scene acceptance and cost per accepted second. P2: website import, multilingual variants, agency collaboration, Shopify and performance integrations. P3: influencer workflows, discovery and advanced editor.

07  Budget and gates

Rs 20,000 ceiling; domain already owned. Rs 7,000 AI experiments; Rs 3,500 demo assets; Rs 3,000 acquisition; Rs 1,500 development tools; Rs 1,000 infrastructure; Rs 4,000 reserve. Release Rs 3,000 first; next Rs 5,000 after three convincing demos; next Rs 8,000 after three paid pilots; preserve Rs 4,000 reserve. Founder labor excluded. No paid ads until organic purchase evidence. Week 1 proof; week 2 paid assisted pilots; weeks 3–5 self-service; week 6 beta. Halt if quality or margin gates fail.
